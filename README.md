    <h1>TenantSnap Quick Reference Guide</h1>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <p class="question">What is TenantSnap?</p>
      <p class="answer">TenantSnap helps tenants and landlords document a property’s condition with photos, notes, checklists, and inspection reports.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <p class="question">How do I start an inspection?</p>
      <p class="answer">Sign in, choose the property or inspection type, then work through each room’s checklist.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <p class="question">Can I add photos to an inspection?</p>
      <p class="answer">Yes. You can take photos or choose existing images to show the condition of rooms and items.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <p class="question">Can I add comments to photos or rooms?</p>
      <p class="answer">Yes. Add notes to explain damage, maintenance concerns, or any important details.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <p class="question">How is the inspection report created?</p>
      <p class="answer">TenantSnap combines your completed checklist, photos, and comments into a PDF inspection report.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <p class="question">Can I download or share my report?</p>
      <p class="answer">Yes. You can download the PDF report and share it by email or another available sharing option on your device.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <p class="question">Where can I find previous reports?</p>
      <p class="answer">Open the History or Reports area from the dashboard to view previously saved inspections.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <p class="question">Can both tenants and landlords use TenantSnap?</p>
      <p class="answer">Yes. TenantSnap supports both roles, helping each person review and keep a record of property condition.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <p class="question">What should I include in an inspection?</p>
      <p class="answer">Record the condition of walls, floors, doors, windows, fixtures, appliances, and any existing damage.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="faq-item">
      <p class="question">What should I do if I find damage?</p>
      <p class="answer">Take clear photos, add a detailed note, and mark the relevant checklist item so it appears in the final report.</p>
    </div>
